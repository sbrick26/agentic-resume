# STYLE

How you write, so your resume and site sound like you at your most
professional. Build it from writing you did yourself (posts, notes, docs).

## The voice in one paragraph

(Three or four sentences: sentence length, how you open, how you state
numbers, how you end.)

## Rules

1. Start resume bullets with the verb of what you did.
2. One idea per sentence.
3. Numbers exactly as you would say them; mark estimates.
4. Outcome last, and concrete.

## Do and don't

| Don't (generic) | Do (you) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Spearheaded a cross-functional initiative | Built X; it cut Y from 9 to 2 | Your verb, real number |

## Words to avoid

spearheaded, leveraged, synergy, passionate, rockstar, world-class,
cutting-edge, revolutionary, seamlessly, utilize, various, numerous.

In the professional voice (resume, site, review messages) also: ngl, lol,
memes.
