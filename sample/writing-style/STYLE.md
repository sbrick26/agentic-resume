# STYLE (sample person)

## The voice in one paragraph

Short and direct. Starts with what was done, ends with the result and the
number. No hype.

## Rules

1. Start resume bullets with a verb.
2. One idea per sentence.
3. Numbers exactly as measured; mark estimates.
4. Outcome last.

## Do and don't

| Don't (generic) | Do (Alex) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Spearheaded reliability improvements | Cut checkout errors from 4.1 to 0.6 percent | Real verb, real number |
| Passionate about developer experience | Wrote a CLI that cut setup from about 2 days to 2 hours | Shows it |

## Words to avoid

spearheaded, leveraged, synergy, passionate, rockstar, world-class,
cutting-edge, revolutionary, seamlessly, utilize, various, numerous.

In the professional voice (resume, site, review messages) also: ngl, lol,
memes.
