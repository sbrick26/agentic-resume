## Situation
Six engineers shared on-call and the noisiest alerts woke someone up most nights.

## What I did
Ran an alert review, deleted or merged 40 alerts, and set a weekly handoff with a one-page checklist.

## Outcome
Pages fell from 14 to 3 a week, and the handoff became the team standard.
